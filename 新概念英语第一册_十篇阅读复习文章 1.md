## 01. My Family（我的家庭）

**语法重点：** be 动词 am / is / are；人称代词；形容词性物主代词 my / his / her。

### English

Hello! My name is Jack. I am a student. I am twenty years old. I am from England, but I live in a small town now.

This is my family. My father is a doctor. He is very busy, but he is kind. My mother is a teacher. She is nice and friendly. I have one sister. Her name is Lucy. She is twelve years old. She is a student, too.

We are a happy family. We are not rich, but we are happy. Our house is small, but it is clean. I love my family very much.

### 阅读理解

1. What is Jack?
2. Where is Jack from?
3. What does his father do?
4. How old is Lucy?
5. Is Jack's family happy?

## 02. My Daily Life（我的日常生活）

**语法重点：** 一般现在时；第三人称单数；频率副词。

### English

My name is Tom. I am a student. I get up at seven o'clock every morning. I wash my face and brush my teeth. Then I have breakfast.

I usually have some bread and milk for breakfast. At eight o'clock, I go to school by bus. My school is not far from my home.

I have four classes in the morning and two classes in the afternoon. I like English because it is interesting. My English teacher speaks slowly, and she is very kind.

I go home at five o'clock. In the evening, I do my homework. Sometimes I read a book or watch TV. I go to bed at ten o'clock.

This is my daily life. It is simple, but I like it.
### 阅读理解

1. What time does Tom get up?
2. How does he go to school?
3. Why does he like English?
4. What does he do in the evening?
5. What time does he go to bed?

## 03. A Busy Day（忙碌的一天）

**语法重点：** 时间表达；一般现在时；and / but / then 连接句子。

### English

Today is Monday. It is a busy day for Anna.

She gets up at six thirty. She is a little tired because she goes to bed late on Sunday. But she gets up quickly and has breakfast.

At seven thirty, she leaves home. She walks to the bus stop and takes a bus to work. She works in a big office. There are many people in the office.

At twelve o'clock, she has lunch with her friends. They usually eat rice, vegetables and chicken. After lunch, they drink some tea and talk about their work.

Anna finishes work at six o'clock. She goes to a shop and buys some food. Then she goes home and cooks dinner.

After dinner, she listens to music and reads a book. She is tired, but she is happy. It is a long day!
### 阅读理解

1. What day is it?
2. Why is Anna a little tired?
3. Where does Anna work?
4. What does she have for lunch?
5. What does she do after dinner?

## 04. At the Shop（在商店里）

**语法重点：** 可数名词与不可数名词；some / any；How much / How many。

### English

It is Saturday morning. Peter goes to a small shop near his home. He wants to buy some food for his family.

There are many things in the shop. There are apples, oranges, bananas and eggs. There is also some milk, bread and rice.

Peter asks the shop assistant, "How much is the milk?"

"It is three pounds," the assistant says.

"How much are these apples?"

"They are two pounds a kilo."

Peter buys a bottle of milk, a loaf of bread, six eggs and some apples. He also wants some coffee, but there isn't any coffee in the shop.

He pays for the food and goes home. His mother is happy because he buys everything they need.
### 阅读理解

1. When does Peter go shopping?
2. What does he want to buy?
3. How much is the milk?
4. Does the shop have any coffee?
5. Why is Peter's mother happy?

## 05. My Room（我的房间）

**语法重点：** There is / There are；方位介词；名词单复数。

### English

This is my bedroom. It is not very big, but it is comfortable.

There is a bed near the window. There is a small table next to the bed. On the table, there is a lamp and a book. There are two pictures on the wall. One picture is of my family, and the other is of a beautiful city.

There is a chair in front of the desk. My computer is on the desk. I use it to study English and write emails.

There are some clothes in the wardrobe. My shoes are under the bed. I always keep my room clean because I like a tidy room.

I spend a lot of time here. It is my favourite place in the house.
### 阅读理解

1. Is the bedroom very big?
2. Where is the lamp?
3. How many pictures are there on the wall?
4. What does the writer use the computer for?
5. Where are the shoes?

## 06. A Good Friend（一位好朋友）

**语法重点：** 形容词；物主代词；like / love；because 引导原因。

### English

I have a good friend. His name is David. He is twenty years old, and he is a university student.

David is tall and thin. He has short black hair and big eyes. He is very friendly and helpful. He always helps other people.

We are in the same English class. His English is better than mine, so he often helps me with my English. I help him with computer lessons.

We have different hobbies. I like reading books, but David likes playing football. He also enjoys listening to music. Sometimes we go to the cinema together.

I like David because he is a good friend. I think a good friend is not only someone who has fun with you, but also someone who helps you when you need help.
### 阅读理解

1. How old is David?
2. What does David look like?
3. What does David help the writer with?
4. What are their hobbies?
5. Why does the writer like David?

## 07. What Are They Doing?（他们正在做什么？）

**语法重点：** 现在进行时；be + 动词-ing；一般现在时与现在进行时的区别。

### English

It is Sunday afternoon. The weather is fine, and the sun is shining.

There are many people in the park. Some children are playing football. Two girls are riding their bikes. A little boy is running after a dog. His mother is sitting under a tree and reading a book.

Near the lake, an old man is feeding the ducks. A young couple are taking photos. They are very happy.

My friends and I are also in the park. I am drinking some water. Jack is talking to Mary, and Peter is playing with a small dog.

We often come here on Sundays. We like this park because it is quiet and beautiful. We are having a good time today.
### 阅读理解

1. What is the weather like?
2. What are the two girls doing?
3. Where is the old man?
4. What is Jack doing?
5. Why do they like the park?

## 08. Yesterday（昨天）

**语法重点：** 一般过去时；was / were；规则与常见不规则动词。

### English

Yesterday was a very interesting day. I got up early because I wanted to visit my grandfather.

After breakfast, I went to the bus station. I took a bus to a small village. The journey was long, but the weather was beautiful.

My grandfather was very happy to see me. He was in the garden. He planted some flowers and worked in the garden every morning.

We had lunch together. My grandmother cooked chicken, rice and vegetables. The food was delicious.

In the afternoon, I helped my grandfather clean the house. Then we sat in the garden and talked about my school life.

I went home at six o'clock. I was tired, but I had a wonderful day. I hope I can visit them again soon.
### 阅读理解

1. Why did the writer get up early?
2. Where did the writer go?
3. How did the writer travel to the village?
4. What did the grandmother cook?
5. What did the writer do in the afternoon?

## 09. A Weekend Trip（一次周末旅行）

**语法重点：** 一般过去时与一般将来时；be going to；will 的初步认识。

### English

Last Saturday, my friends and I went to the seaside. We wanted to have a short holiday.

We left home early in the morning and arrived at the beach at ten o'clock. The sea was blue, and the sky was clear. There were many people on the beach.

Some people were swimming in the sea. Others were lying in the sun. My friend David took many photos. I walked along the beach and collected some beautiful shells.

At noon, we had lunch in a small restaurant. The food was good, and the people were friendly.

We came home in the evening. We were very tired, but we enjoyed the trip.

Next month, we are going to visit a mountain. We will take some food and water with us. I think it will be another wonderful trip!
### 阅读理解

1. Where did the writer go last Saturday?
2. What was the weather like?
3. What did David do?
4. What did the writer collect?
5. Where are they going next month?

## 10. An Interesting Story（一个有趣的故事）

**语法重点：** 多种时态综合；情态动词 can / must；because / when / but。

### English

One morning, a young boy named Ben was walking to school. He was carrying a schoolbag and a small box.

On his way to school, he saw a little dog near a tree. The dog was hungry and cold. It looked at Ben and made a small noise.

Ben stopped and looked at the dog. "Are you hungry?" he asked.

He opened his box. There was a sandwich inside. Ben gave the sandwich to the dog. The dog ate it quickly and looked very happy.

Ben wanted to take the dog home, but he had to go to school. He called his father and told him about the dog. His father said, "You can take it home after school. I will help you."

After school, Ben went back to the tree. The dog was still there. Ben took it home. His family gave the dog some food and water.

Now the dog lives with Ben's family. They call it Lucky. Ben takes it for a walk every day. He is very happy because he has a new friend.
### 阅读理解

1. What was Ben carrying?
2. Where did Ben see the dog?
3. Why did Ben not take the dog home at first?
4. Who helped Ben?
5. What does Ben do every day?
