## **01. The Power of Good Habits（良好习惯的力量）**

Many people dream of becoming successful, but few of them understand the importance of daily habits. Success does not usually happen overnight. Instead, it is the result of small actions that we repeat every day.

For example, reading for thirty minutes each morning may not seem important at first. However, after several months, you will have finished many books and learned a great deal of new information.

The same principle applies to exercise, language learning and other activities. If you want to improve your English, you should spend some time practising every day rather than studying for several hours only once a week.

Another important habit is learning how to manage your time. Many people waste hours on social media without realizing how quickly time passes.

Building good habits requires patience and determination. You may not notice any immediate changes, but your efforts will eventually produce results.

Remember that a better future is created by what you do today.

2. What happens if you read for thirty minutes every morning?
    
3. Why do people need to manage their time?
    
4. What qualities are necessary for building good habits?
    

## **02. A Journey That Changed My Life（一次改变我人生的旅行）**

Several years ago, I decided to take a journey alone. At that time, I was a shy person who rarely talked to strangers. I preferred staying at home because I was afraid of making mistakes or getting into difficult situations.

One summer, I bought a train ticket to a small town in the mountains. I had never visited that place before, and I knew almost nothing about it.

During the journey, I met an elderly man who was travelling to the same destination. He told me interesting stories about his hometown and recommended several places that I should visit.

When I arrived, I discovered that the town was much more beautiful than I had expected. The local people were friendly, and the natural scenery was amazing.

Although I sometimes had trouble communicating with others, I gradually became more confident.

That experience taught me an important lesson: we should not allow fear to prevent us from exploring the world. Sometimes, the most valuable experiences happen when we step outside our comfort zone.

2. Who did the writer meet on the train?
    
3. How did the journey influence the writer?
    
4. What does "step outside our comfort zone" mean?
    

## **03. The Importance of Learning from Failure（从失败中学习的重要性）**

Failure is something that everyone experiences at some point in life. However, different people have different attitudes towards it. Some people become disappointed and lose confidence, while others regard failure as an opportunity to improve themselves.

When I was younger, I was afraid of making mistakes. Whenever I received a poor result in an examination, I felt embarrassed and believed that I was not intelligent enough.

One day, my teacher told me something that completely changed my attitude. She said, "A mistake is not the end of your journey. It is simply a chance to discover what you need to improve."

Since then, I have tried to pay more attention to my weaknesses instead of worrying about my results.

For instance, when I find a difficult subject, I divide it into several smaller parts and study them one by one. This method makes complicated problems easier to understand.

Failure may be painful, but it can also provide valuable lessons. The most important thing is not how many times we fall, but whether we are willing to stand up again.

## **04. Technology and Our Daily Lives（科技与我们的日常生活）**

Technology has changed the way people live, work and communicate. In the past, people had to visit libraries to find information or write letters to communicate with friends who lived far away. Today, almost everything can be done through the Internet.

With a smartphone, we can read the news, watch educational videos, attend online classes and communicate with people from different countries.

Technology has also created many new opportunities. Students can learn new skills without leaving their homes, while businesses can sell their products to customers around the world.

However, modern technology also brings certain problems. Many people spend too much time looking at their screens, which may affect their health and relationships. In addition, false information can spread quickly through social media.

Therefore, we should learn to use technology wisely. It should be a useful tool that helps us solve problems, rather than something that controls our lives.

## **05. Why Reading Is Still Important（为什么阅读仍然重要）**

In the modern world, information is everywhere. We can watch videos, listen to podcasts and read short articles on our phones. As a result, some people believe that reading books is no longer necessary.

However, reading remains one of the most effective ways to develop our knowledge and thinking abilities.

When we read a book, we are not simply collecting information. We are also learning how to understand different ideas, recognize relationships between events and express our own opinions.

Reading can also improve our language skills. By reading different kinds of books, we become familiar with new vocabulary, sentence structures and expressions.

For English learners, this is especially important. Memorizing individual words is useful, but understanding how they are used in sentences is even more valuable.

Of course, reading requires concentration and patience. At first, you may need to look up many unfamiliar words. However, as your vocabulary grows, reading will become easier and more enjoyable.

A good book can be more than a source of information. It can become a teacher, a friend and a window into another world.

## **06. The Art of Managing Money（理财的艺术）**

Many young people begin to manage their own money when they enter university. For some students, this is the first time they have to make financial decisions without their parents' help.

At first, managing money may seem quite simple. You receive a certain amount every month and spend it on food, transportation, entertainment and other needs.

However, problems may appear when you spend more than you earn. Buying unnecessary things, ordering expensive meals and following the latest fashion trends can quickly reduce your savings.

A useful way to avoid these problems is to prepare a monthly budget. Write down your income and expenses, then decide how much money you can spend on different activities.

It is also important to develop the habit of saving. Even if you can only save a small amount each month, the money will gradually increase.

Learning how to manage money is not simply about becoming rich. It is about making sensible decisions, understanding your needs and preparing for unexpected situations.

4. What is the real purpose of learning to manage money?
    

## **07. Protecting the Environment（保护环境）**

Environmental protection has become an important issue in many countries. As the population continues to grow, people consume more natural resources and produce increasing amounts of waste.

Air pollution, water pollution and climate change are serious problems that affect both human beings and wildlife.

In large cities, cars and factories release harmful gases into the atmosphere. These gases can damage the environment and may also cause health problems.

Fortunately, there are several ways in which individuals can contribute to environmental protection. For example, we can use public transportation instead of driving private cars. We can also reduce plastic waste by carrying reusable shopping bags and water bottles.

Governments and companies should also take responsibility. They need to develop cleaner technologies and encourage people to adopt environmentally friendly habits.

Protecting the environment is not only the responsibility of governments. Every individual can make a difference through small but meaningful actions.

If we work together, we can create a cleaner and healthier world for future generations.

## **08. The Future of Artificial Intelligence（人工智能的未来）**

Artificial intelligence, often called AI, is developing rapidly and changing many industries. Today, AI systems can translate languages, recognize pictures, write articles and even help doctors examine patients.

In the past, many of these tasks required a large amount of time and human effort. However, modern AI tools can complete some of them within seconds.

For students, AI provides a new way to learn. They can use AI assistants to explain difficult subjects, correct grammar mistakes and practise foreign languages.

For software developers, AI can help write code, find errors and improve working efficiency. This allows developers to spend more time solving complex problems.

Nevertheless, AI also creates challenges. Some jobs may disappear because machines can perform certain tasks more efficiently. There are also concerns about privacy, security and the reliability of information produced by AI systems.

In my opinion, AI should not completely replace human intelligence. Instead, people should learn how to work with it. Those who understand both technology and human needs may have more opportunities in the future.

## **09. Why Do People Travel?（人们为什么旅行？）**

People travel for different reasons. Some want to relax and escape from their busy lives, while others are interested in discovering new cultures and meeting people from different backgrounds.

Travelling allows us to experience things that we cannot learn from books alone. When we visit another country, we may discover different traditions, lifestyles and ways of thinking.

For example, food is an important part of every culture. Trying local dishes can help us understand the history and customs of a particular place.

Travelling can also teach us to become more independent. When we are in an unfamiliar environment, we need to make decisions, solve problems and communicate with strangers.

Of course, travelling is not always comfortable. Flights may be delayed, hotels may be disappointing and unexpected problems may occur.

However, these experiences often become interesting memories. More importantly, travelling helps us understand that people may have different lifestyles, but they often share similar hopes and feelings.

## **10. The Secret of Continuous Improvement（持续进步的秘诀）**

Many people want to achieve great things, but they often underestimate the value of continuous improvement. They expect immediate results and become frustrated when progress is slower than they imagined.

However, meaningful achievements usually require time, effort and a clear direction.

Consider someone who wants to become fluent in a foreign language. Memorizing hundreds of words in a single day may seem impressive, but most of them will soon be forgotten if they are not reviewed regularly.

A more effective approach is to learn a reasonable number of words every day, practise using them in sentences and review previous lessons at regular intervals.

The same idea applies to almost every skill. Whether you are learning programming, playing an instrument or developing your communication abilities, regular practice is essential.

It is also important to evaluate your progress. Instead of comparing yourself with others, look at what you could do one month ago and what you can do today.

There will always be difficulties along the way. You may feel tired, lose motivation or even want to give up.

But remember: progress does not always happen in a straight line. As long as you continue learning and making adjustments, you are moving forward.

2. Why is memorizing hundreds of words in one day not necessarily effective?
    
3. How should we evaluate our progress?
    
4. What does the writer mean by "progress does not always happen in a straight line"?
